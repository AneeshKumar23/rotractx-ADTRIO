PM Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

About the Scheme

The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme was introduced in 1998 by NABARD to provide farmers with timely access to credit. It offers farmers credit for crops, post-harvest expenses, working capital for farm assets and allied activities, as well as consumption needs. The card helps reduce dependence on informal credit and provides access to institutional credit at subsidized interest rates.

Features

  • Short-term credit support for crops and term loans for agriculture and allied activities.
  • Credit limit based on operational land holding, cropping pattern, and scale of finance.
  • Interest subvention and prompt repayment incentives available.

Interest Rates and Loan Details

  • Base interest rate: ~7% per annum (subject to bank policy)
  • Interest subvention of 2% by the Government of India
  • Additional 3% incentive on timely repayment — effective interest rate can be as low as 4%
  • Loan limit: up to ₹3 lakhs (can vary by bank)
  • Repayment period: typically 12 months, extendable for term loans

Eligibility

  • All farmers: individuals/joint borrowers who are owner-cultivators.
  • Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers.
  • Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers.

Documents Required

  • Application form
  • Land documents
  • KYC documents: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, etc.
  • Passport-size photographs

Benefits

  • Easy access to credit
  • Lower interest rates with subsidy
  • Flexible repayment options
  • Coverage for allied and non-farming activities
