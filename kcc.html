PM Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

About PM Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

PM Kisan Credit Card (KCC) is a scheme launched by the Government of India to provide financial assistance to farmers. Under this scheme, farmers can avail credit facilities at low interest rates for agricultural purposes.

Eligibility Criteria

- Minimum land size: 0.5 acres
- Annual income: ₹5,00,000 or less
- Age limit: Up to 65 years
- Required documents: Aadhaar card, land documents, and KYC details.

Benefits

- Credit at low interest rates
- Quick disbursement
- Suitable for buying seeds, fertilizers, and agricultural machinery
- Credit limit of up to ₹1.6 lakh.
